# Ollie logo, Step

An O cut in two, the right half one step higher: the owner side and the subscriber side, kept strictly apart, and one step up.

## Files

- `svg/` masters: `symbol`, `horizontal`, `stacked`, each in `color` (light backgrounds), `reversed` (dark or Forest backgrounds), `black`, `white`. Text is outlined, no fonts needed.
- `png/` the same at 1024 (symbol), 2000 (horizontal) and 1200 (stacked) px wide, transparent.
- `app-icon/` 1024 px square icon (`.svg` and `.png`), a rounded version for web and marketing, and two transparent layers (`layer-left-half.svg`, `layer-right-half.svg`) for building the Icon Composer `.icon` on a Forest background.
- `web/` `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `favicon.svg`, `apple-touch-icon.png` (180), `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `site.webmanifest`, and `head-snippet.html`.

## Rules

- Clear space: at least the width of the gap between the halves times three on every side.
- Minimum size: symbol 16 px, horizontal lockup 80 px wide.
- Keep the halves, the gap and the step exactly as drawn. Do not rotate, recolour outside the palette, or add effects.
- Wordmark: Geist SemiBold, tracking -3.5%, outlined (SIL Open Font License, logo use allowed).
