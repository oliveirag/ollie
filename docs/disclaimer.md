# Before you connect

**Ollie is not financial advice.** It publishes a generic, timestamped record of
trading signals that one person's rule-based strategy produced and that they
chose to act on in their own account. Nothing here is a recommendation to you,
and nothing here takes your situation, goals, or finances into account.

**Every subscriber sees the same signals.** They are not personalized. The
quantity shown is the size the owner's strategy used; it says nothing about
what size, if any, is appropriate for anyone else.

**Past performance is not indicative of future results.** The track record is a
history of what already happened, computed from records that cannot be edited.
It is not a forecast, and a strategy that worked can stop working at any time.

**You act at your own risk, through your own account, on your own decisions.**
Ollie never holds your brokerage credentials, never sees your account, and
never places, modifies, or cancels an order for you. If you connect an agent to
this feed, that agent is yours, its decisions are yours, and every trade it
makes is your responsibility alone.

**You can lose money.** Trading securities involves risk, including the loss of
your entire investment.

**Ollie is a publisher, not an adviser.** It is not registered as an investment
adviser or broker-dealer, and it does not manage money for anyone.

By accepting, you confirm that you have read this, that you understand it, and
that you will not rely on Ollie as advice.
